X-ray Blackjack

Make your image match the IDEAL as closely as you can, using as little dose as possible.
1Both teams start from the same grainy image. The ideal image stays on screen.
2Each round your team plays one multiplier card. It multiplies your exposure: new image, more dose. Both teams see every image.
3No copying: the card the first team plays is blocked for the second team that round. The first pick alternates.
4Up to 4 rounds. Say STAND when you think you are close enough. The other team keeps playing its remaining rounds, so standing early gains nothing.
5Your last image is your submission, not your best one.

Scoring

Score = Quality × 2 − Dose
Quality stays hidden until the end: judge it with your eyes. Higher score wins.
