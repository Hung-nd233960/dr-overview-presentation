## Activity dry-run (do twice, with a timer)

1. **Warm-up** (1:00): C asks lowest, D reveals; C asks highest, D reveals; punchline.
2. **Blackjack** (2:30): split room, Random picks the first team, 4 rounds ≈ 25 s each, Reveal, punchline. Practise the "stand" and the "undo" key. The start is random every game (0.08–0.16× of ideal), so there is no fixed best route; for a predictable rehearsal open `DR_Lab.html?tab=bj&r0=0.12` (best route ×1.5 then ×6 = 180.2; ×3 then ×3 = 178.4).
3. Check the debrief on slide 12 follows within 10 s.

## Backup plan

- **HTML won't open**: slides 10 and 11 contain the four warm-up images and the start/ideal pair. Run Activity 1 from the slide; run Blackjack verbally with printed cards (×⅙ … ×6, including ×1.5) and a hand tally of dose (cost = 10 × exposure of that scan + 3; cards chain on the last scan).
- **No projector colour**: dose bar colours are also labelled by position (green/amber/red = 50% / 85%).
- **Class too small for two teams**: one team of the whole room plays against the ideal; skip "no copy".
- **Out of time**: shorten Blackjack to 3 rounds; cut slide 13 to one sentence.
